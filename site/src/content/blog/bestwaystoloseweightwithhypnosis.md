Hypnosis for weight loss is one of the most searched-for topics in the hypnotherapy world — and also one of the most misunderstood. Let me try to separate what works from what doesn't, based on my clinical experience and the available research.

**What hypnosis for weight loss is not:**
It is not a magic solution that makes weight fall off without any engagement from you. It is not a one-session fix. It is not about turning off your appetite permanently or making food feel disgusting.

**What it is:**
Hypnosis for weight loss is the use of a deeply focused, highly receptive mental state to access and change the subconscious patterns that drive eating behaviour. It is most effective when it addresses the full picture — not just appetite, but the emotional, habitual, and belief-based drivers of how and why you eat.

**The approaches that work best:**

*1. Addressing emotional eating.* Identifying the specific emotional triggers that lead to eating outside hunger — stress, boredom, loneliness, reward, punishment — and creating new, healthier responses to those triggers at the subconscious level.

*2. Changing body image.* The beliefs we hold about our bodies are powerfully self-fulfilling. Hypnotherapy works to shift these beliefs — not to unrealistic ideals, but to a more accurate and compassionate self-perception.

*3. Building a different relationship with food.* Moving from an adversarial, guilt-laden relationship with food to one of curiosity, pleasure, and genuine nourishment.

*4. Self-hypnosis as a maintenance practice.* Teaching the client to use self-hypnosis daily is one of the most effective things I do — it gives you a tool that keeps working long after our sessions end.

*5. Combining with practical support.* Hypnotherapy works best alongside good nutritional guidance and, where appropriate, medical input. It is a complement, not a replacement.
