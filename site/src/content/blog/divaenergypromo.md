When I started teaching singing over thirty-five years ago, I had no idea I was building towards something called Diva Energy®. I was just trying to help people find their voices.

But over the years, a pattern emerged. The most powerful transformations I witnessed weren't about technique. They weren't about breath support or vowel placement or any of the many technical elements of the singing voice. They were about *belief*. About the moment a person stopped thinking of themselves as someone who couldn't sing and started thinking of themselves as someone who could.

Diva Energy® grew out of that insight. It is the name I've given to that state — the state of vocal confidence, authentic presence, and genuine belief that your voice deserves to be heard. And it is the goal of everything I do.

This November, I'm excited to share some news about where Diva Energy® is going next. New programmes, new ways to work together, and a new vision for what becomes possible when we combine world-class vocal technique with the transformational power of clinical hypnotherapy.

If you're already part of the Diva world — thank you. Your trust and your commitment to your voice is what makes all of this possible.

If you're new here — welcome. There has never been a better time to start.

Keep an eye on your inbox. The next chapter is about to begin.
