The boardroom has its own acoustics. The conference room, the networking event, the live stage, the Zoom call — each one demands something specific from the voice of the person speaking. And most women I meet have never been taught what that is.

This is not a minor professional gap. Your voice is the primary instrument through which your ideas, your authority, and your credibility are communicated. If your voice trembles when you're under pressure, loses volume when you're challenged, or rises to a pitch that signals uncertainty rather than confidence — it doesn't matter how excellent your content is.

Research consistently shows that vocal characteristics influence perceived competence, authority, and trustworthiness. Not because we're shallow, but because we're human. We evolved to read voice as a signal of safety, capability, and intention.

The good news is that every single vocal characteristic that undermines your professional presence can be changed. Breath support. Resonance. Pace. Articulation. The nervous system regulation that stops your voice from betraying your anxiety at the worst possible moment.

This is exactly what the BRAVO for Speakers programme addresses. Built on the same principles as the vocal work I do with professional singers, BRAVO applies the science of the voice to the professional context — helping you develop a voice that commands rooms, holds attention, and sounds exactly like the leader you are.
