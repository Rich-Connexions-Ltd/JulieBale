If you've ever tried and failed at a diet, I want you to read this carefully. The diet industry has a long-term success rate of approximately 5%. That means 95% of people who go on a diet regain the weight within five years. Many regain more than they lost.

This is not a secret. It is well-documented in decades of scientific research. And yet the $70 billion global diet industry continues to sell the same products, the same promises, and the same narrative: that if you failed, you didn't try hard enough.

This is a lie. A profitable one, because a product that worked wouldn't need to be bought again.

The reason diets fail is not lack of willpower or commitment. It is that they operate on the wrong level. They address what you eat, not *why* you eat. They focus on conscious behaviour, while the patterns that drive eating — the emotional associations, the habits, the comfort responses — are subconscious.

You cannot think your way out of patterns that exist below the level of conscious thought. You can only change them by working at the level where they live.

This is why hypnotherapy approaches to eating and weight are so effective for people who have "tried everything." Not because they're magic, but because they operate at the correct level — the subconscious — where the patterns actually are.

If you've been blaming yourself for diet failures, please stop. The model was broken. You weren't.

What you need isn't more willpower. It's a different approach — one that works with your mind rather than against it.
