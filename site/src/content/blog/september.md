There is something about September that feels like a fresh start. The summer looseness fades, the days get crisper, and something in us wants to begin again. New term energy. New resolve. The sense that now — finally — is the time.

I've been teaching long enough to know that September is when a particular kind of phone call or email arrives. From people who spent all summer telling themselves they'd think about it later. Who had the idea in July but weren't quite ready. Who needed the peculiar forward momentum of autumn to finally act.

I love those emails. I love that moment when someone decides they're ready.

If you're reading this and recognising yourself — the person who has been *meaning* to do something about your voice for a while — this is your September nudge. Not a sales pitch. Just a genuine observation: the people who act when the feeling is freshest are usually the ones who transform fastest.

The From First Note to Final Curtain programme begins again in September. It is a six-month (or twelve-month) journey through every dimension of the singing voice — technique, performance, confidence, and mindset. It culminates in a real gala concert. It has changed the lives of over a thousand people.

There are always limited places. And September, in my experience, fills up fast.

If you've been meaning to get in touch — now would be a very good time.
