Every week I receive questions about hypnotherapy. What is it, really? Is it safe? Can anyone be hypnotised? Will I lose control? Will I remember what happened?

I've answered these questions so many times that I thought it was time to put them all in one place.

**Will I be in control?** Yes, always. Contrary to the stage hypnosis stereotype, clinical hypnotherapy does not allow a therapist to make you do anything against your will or values. You are in a relaxed, focused state — not unconscious, not under someone's power. You can end the session at any time.

**Will I remember what happened?** In most clinical hypnotherapy sessions, yes. You will be aware of what is happening and will remember it. Some clients enter very deep states and have patchy recall, but this is not the norm.

**Can anyone be hypnotised?** Most people can enter at least a light hypnotic state. The small percentage who cannot are usually those with certain neurological conditions or who are highly resistant to relaxation. For the vast majority of people, hypnosis is straightforward.

**How many sessions will I need?** This depends entirely on the issue. Some clients see significant change in two or three sessions. Others benefit from longer programmes. I am always honest about this in an initial consultation.

**Is it safe?** When practised by a qualified, registered clinical hypnotherapist, yes. I am registered with the NCH (National Council for Hypnotherapy) and the CNHC (Complementary and Natural Healthcare Council).

If you have a question I haven't answered here, I'd love to hear from you.
