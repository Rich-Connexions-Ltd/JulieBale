An audition is a fascinating psychological environment. In the space of a few minutes, you are being asked to perform your best while being directly observed and evaluated by people whose opinion matters enormously to you. Everything that can go wrong with the voice under pressure is likely to go wrong in that room.

And yet some people nail auditions consistently. Not because they're always technically superior to the competition — although they may be — but because they have learned to perform at their best precisely when the stakes are highest.

This is a mental skill. And like all mental skills, it can be trained.

**The pre-audition process matters as much as the preparation.** Most singers spend all their preparation time on the technical and performance elements and almost none on the mental state they want to be in when they walk through the door. Clinical hypnotherapy can help you develop and anchor a specific state of calm, focused, energised confidence that you can access reliably before any audition.

**Anticipatory anxiety is the real problem.** It's not the audition itself that derails most singers — it's the two weeks beforehand. The catastrophising. The sleepless rehearsal runs at 3am. The spiral of imagining failure. Hypnotherapy specifically addresses anticipatory anxiety, replacing the catastrophic imagination with a rehearsed, compelling vision of success.

**The body remembers.** Through repeated hypnotic rehearsal of a successful audition, you build neural pathways that make the real performance feel familiar. The nerves are still there — but they're navigating known territory.

**The script matters.** What you tell yourself about auditions — and about yourself — shapes your experience of them. Identifying and rewriting the internal narrative that says "I always freeze in auditions" is one of the most transformational pieces of work I do with singers preparing for high-stakes performance.

If auditions are your nemesis, I'd love to help you make them your strength instead.
