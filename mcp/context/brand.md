# Brand

## Who Julie is
Julie Bale is a dramatic soprano, Vocal Transformation Specialist and Mentor. She
has spent more than 35 years singing, performing, teaching and being fascinated by
what happens when somebody finds their voice. She still performs, and still loves
it. She works with adults: complete beginners, returning singers, choir singers
who have never sung alone, and people who were told years ago that they couldn't.

## Diva Energy®
The world, community and philosophy around Julie's work. For Julie, Diva Energy is
about allowing yourself to step forward, take your voice seriously, enjoy being
seen and heard, stop apologising for wanting something, and sometimes just put on
a fabulous dress and sing.

## Brand hierarchy
- **Julie Bale** — the public master brand.
- **Diva Energy®** — the world and community.
- **From First Note to Final Curtain** — the flagship mentorship.
- **What's Your Singing Story?** — the podcast.
- **Hope Sings Eternal** — the recording/performance project.

## Feel
Editorial, theatrical, warm, sophisticated, spacious, confident, personal. Like an
arts journal or concert programme, not online-course software or a coaching
template. Palette: deep teal, warm cream, antique gold (gold used sparingly like
jewellery), near-black text. Fonts: Playfair Display headings, Raleway body.

## The point of the homepage
Make the right visitor think: "I think this woman understands me", then "I would
love to be part of this world", then "perhaps I really could sing." The deeper
pages carry the detail.
