About Julie

I’m a singer first

I’m Julie Bale, a dramatic soprano, Vocal Transformation Specialist and Mentor. I’ve spent more than 35 years singing, performing, teaching and being fascinated by what happens when somebody finds their voice. And I still absolutely love it.

You don’t need to call yourself a singer. Not yet, anyway.
